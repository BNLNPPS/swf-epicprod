What this is

When the catalog of record will not take an output — the door unreachable, the catalog refusing — the job writes it to a BNL dCache space and exits on its physics, rather than losing finished work for want of a destination. The drain catalogues each stashed file at the stash RSE with what it owes, and brings it home when JLab answers. Nothing listed here is lost; it is owed.
