What this is

When the catalog of record will not take an output — the door unreachable, the catalog refusing — the job writes it to a BNL dCache space and exits on its physics, rather than losing finished work for want of a destination. The drain catalogues each stashed file at the stash RSE with what it owes and, when JLab answers, brings it home: a copy to the destination RSE at the path its logical name maps to, verified by size and checksum, registered in the catalog of record with its event count, and only then removed from the stash. A file that will not move is tried again hourly, a bounded number of times, with its last error shown here. Nothing listed here is lost; it is owed.
